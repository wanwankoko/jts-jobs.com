Worker Dispatch Services (Hakken)

We dispatch foreign staff to worksites such as hotel housekeeping and warehouse sorting and picking, mainly across Tokyo and Chiba. We respond quickly to peak-season ramp-ups and sudden vacancies.

Strengths of JTS Dispatch

  • A ready-to-work talent pool: Many staff are registered with us, centered on part-time workers on student and dependent (family) visas. From a few days a week or short shifts to full-time, we propose staff matched to each site's needs.
  • Experience managing large-scale sites: Our representative has experience managing more than 300 dispatched staff at a logistics center sorting site. Even on sites with large headcounts, we handle attendance management and shift coordination reliably.
  • A staffing company that knows the workplace: We also operate our own restaurant, so we understand the reality of workplaces where foreign staff work. We value communication with both client companies and staff, which leads to better retention.

Main Dispatch Destinations and Work

  • Hotel and facility cleaning (guest room cleaning, common-area cleaning)
  • Logistics warehouses (sorting, picking, inspection, packing)
  • Factory lines, food processing assistance, and more

Our main service areas are Tokyo, Chiba and Kanagawa. Please feel free to ask about other areas as well.

How It Works

STEP 1

Request & consultation

Tell us the headcount, period and type of work you need.

STEP 2

Contract & staff selection

We conclude a worker dispatch master agreement and individual contracts, and select staff who meet your conditions.

STEP 3

Work begins

Dispatched staff start work. We accompany them on the first day and coordinate with the site.

STEP 4

Ongoing follow-up

We continue to operate in line with the law, including attendance and shift management and maintaining the dispatch destination management ledger.
