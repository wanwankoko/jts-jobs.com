Specified Skilled Worker (Placement & Registered Support)

We handle everything consistently, from introducing Specified Skilled Worker (SSW / Tokuteigino) talent to designing and implementing support plans as a Registered Support Organization. Leave residence-status checks and the various procedures to us as well.

Fields We Cover

We cover a wide range of fields, including agriculture / food service / food and beverage manufacturing / nursing care / automobile transport. We mainly introduce talent already living in Japan — current SSW holders looking to change jobs, technical intern trainees who have completed their program, and international students wishing to switch to SSW status.

Support as a Registered Support Organization

  • Designing and implementing support plans (statutory support items such as pre-entry guidance, airport pick-up, assistance securing housing, and life orientation)
  • Regular interviews and consultation support, including in workers' native languages
  • Accompanying and guiding workers through administrative procedures, and providing information on Japanese-language learning opportunities

🚚 Focus field: Automobile transport (truck drivers)

We introduce Specified Skilled Worker drivers to a transport industry facing a severe driver shortage, and support their retention. (Under the SSW system, the automobile transport field is a direct-employment system — it is not dispatch.)

The biggest hurdle is converting to a Japanese license (foreign license conversion). Since October 2025, the knowledge check has been tightened to 50 questions with a 90% correct-answer requirement, making the road to obtaining a license longer. At JTS, we support the whole process consistently — from checking a candidate's license status in advance, to managing the schedule through conversion and higher-class license acquisition, to guidance on exam preparation — so that we prevent the situation of "we hired them, but they can't get a license."

About the logistics warehouse field: The "logistics warehouse" field — covering work such as sorting and picking inside warehouses — was added to the Specified Skilled Worker fields by a Cabinet decision in January 2026. At present, the intake system for the field, including the establishment of the field-specific council required for acceptance, is still being prepared. Drawing on the logistics-site experience gained through our dispatch business, JTS is preparing to begin placement for direct employment and registered support. Please contact us for details.
